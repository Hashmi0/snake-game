# Direction contract

User-approved world: a tactile garden toy, with a sculpted 3D game as the signature. Mode: experience. Follow the approved cream-and-green garden direction; a concept roll was attempted previously but cannot override this explicit brief. Implementation is procedural/code-led as requested. No generated imagery is required.

Palette: linen #f4f2e5, forest #263e2d, leaf #527342, moss #9db678, butter #e7ecb8, fruit coral #df624d. Typeface roles: Lilita One for expressive compact display lettering; Nunito Sans for clear controls and body. Self-host package assets. Tabular numerals for score.

Desktop: quiet masthead; two-column stage with expressive headline, speed selector and primary action on left, larger sculpted board and HUD on right. Mobile: compact masthead, board-first after a short title, compact actions and direction pad below; landscape uses a side rail. Game remains first-viewport content, not a landing-page demo. Controls outside board. Ready/paused/end state copy lives in the action panel rather than covering play.

The memorable element is the living rounded snake on a raised garden board, with soft contact shadows and a sculpted fruit. Surrounding chrome remains quiet. Avoid ornamental cards, gradients, noisy decorative statistics, and long marketing sections. Required game scores are functional HUD content.

Quality floor: board completely visible at required sizes; no clipping/overlap; 44px touch controls; reduced motion; focus states; muted/audio states; readable errors; score numerals stable. Take one combined desktop/mobile inspection batch, fix substantive issues, then confirm.
